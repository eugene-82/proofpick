# Core Reliability Invariants

These invariants protect the evidence-to-decision path before counter-evidence search is added.

- Independence is explicit and deterministic. Raw-content length alone never promotes a source: CONFIRMED means a structurally substantive full SearchResult document has distinct provenance and no known alias, exact-content, or strong-copy dependency. Semantic support still requires a VERIFIED relation. Exact or strong body copies are DEPENDENT, uncertain sources remain UNKNOWN, and only confirmed dependency groups increase independent support.
- Source preservation is separate from independence. Similarity alone never deletes evidence: changes to polarity/negation, material measurements, author/experiencer, or claim-bearing observation context preserve both sources. Generic claim text, product names, or one shared number are not copy proof.
- Source and group IDs come from one analysis-local SourceIdentityRegistry. Internal handles remain stable while public source/group identities, richer representatives, URL aliases, fingerprints, and dependency states are recomputed from the canonical final source set, so all-at-once and incremental arrival permutations converge.
- Only claims marked VERIFIED by the structured semantic verification provider may enter a decision-driving snapshot. The provider owns subject/entity, predicate/action, polarity, experiencer/directness, hypothetical status, target-product relation, and observation type. Deterministic grounding validates exact source/product/quote provenance and span eligibility, never upgrades UNCERTAIN, and sends repair attempts through the same verifier.
- Claim-core validity is separate from optional metadata validity. Unsupported usage_period_months is removed and reported as a metadata issue without discarding an otherwise grounded claim.
- Compression is deterministic and extractive. It covers document regions, preserves positive counter-evidence and risks together, redistributes unused budget, and avoids cutting negation context. Internally clipped segments remain diagnostic only: only complete safe spans are grounding-eligible decision evidence, and evidence_coverage_limited propagates to confidence when unsafe partial content exists.
- Confidence carries evidence quality, verified-claim coverage, compression coverage, and semantic observation horizon. Snippet-only mass, any UNKNOWN-quality decision input, unsafe partial evidence, no verified coverage, and first-impression-only durability support cannot produce an overconfident BUY. Only provider-labeled USAGE/OWNERSHIP/TEST relations can establish a horizon; WARRANTY/SUBSCRIPTION/RETURN_PERIOD/HYPOTHETICAL cannot, and long-term observation is not required for non-durability claims.
- Unresolved severe risks are computed before decision branching and retained on every applicable return path. Positive support and insufficient-evidence early returns cannot erase them.
- Complete-link clustering keeps the configured threshold unchanged and first canonicalizes claim order. The same claim set therefore produces the same clusters and downstream decision regardless of caller order.
- EvaluationSnapshot is deeply immutable, while snapshot-aware clustering, confidence, and decision service boundaries revalidate the full model and lineage regardless of construction path. Its digest binds analysis, stable product identity, registry owner/revision/manifest, source/group identity, normalized/original URL and content hash, evidence content/segments/coverage/quality, and decision-driving claim semantics, sentiment, severity, grounding, and provenance. ClusterResult also binds the snapshot/claim/provenance manifests and validates member aggregates; ConfidenceResult binds the cluster and is deterministically recomputed before decision.
- Severity is aggregated once per independence group using the group's maximum observed severity. Copied URLs cannot add weight, and a minor report cannot dilute repeated severe reports.
- BUY requires sufficient evidence plus affirmative independently supported claims, with no blocking issue or unresolved major risk/conflict. Neutral-only evidence never defaults to BUY.

Numeric threshold and product-category calibration remain deferred to TASK 019. Counter-evidence search and persistence remain deferred to TASK 012 and later tasks.
